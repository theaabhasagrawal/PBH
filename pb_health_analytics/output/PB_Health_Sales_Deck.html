Objection Battlecards

Page 2: Word-for-word counters & quick reference
Listen → Acknowledge → Answer → Move on
1Kahan hai? Bahut door hai.
Customer: "Kahan pe hai yeh hospital?" (110 calls - by far the most asked)
You: "MG Road, Gurugram. Abhi pehla hospital yahi hai; Noida aur Delhi next. Claim ke time PB ka RM 30 minute mein hospital pahunchta hai."
► Only MG Road is open - never say Noida, Delhi or Faridabad is.
2Hospital hai ya clinic?
Customer: "That's a hospital also ya phir koi walk-in facility?"
You: "100-bed multispeciality hospital: modular OT, critical care, MRI, CT, Cath Lab. Consultation se surgery aur admission tak."
► Say 'OT, critical care' explicitly - never 'clinic' or 'OPD card'.
3Discount kitna milega?
Customer: "Matlab discount kitna mil jayega?"
You: "PB customer ko day one se: consultation pe 50% off, tests aur scans pe 20% off."
► Only 50% / 20%. Not 25%, 30%, 75% or 'free'.
4Doctors kaun hain?
Customer: "Yahan ke MD kaun hai? Max jaisa hai?"
You: "Apollo, Max, Narayana, Manipal aur Armed Forces ke senior doctors. Salaried hain, commission nahi."
► Same senior expertise + no billing incentive.
5Meri policy chalegi?
Customer: "Meri policy wahan cashless chalegi?"
You: "Cashless process Policybazaar ke saath integrated hai. Apni policy ka naam bataiye, main abhi confirm karta hoon."
► Never guess - confirm the insurer live.
6Zarurat nahi, aage chalo
Customer: "Mujhe nahi chahiye, hospital mein mujhe nahi jaana."
You: "Bilkul sir. Bas yaad rakhiye: hospital, doctor aur claim advisor, teeno PB ke. Location WhatsApp kar deta hoon."
► Say it once, then move on.
7Insurance company ka hospital - wahin bhejoge?
Customer: "Aap apne hi hospital mein bhejna chahte ho na?"
You: "Bilkul nahi sir - aapki policy saare network hospitals mein valid hai, choice aapki hai. PB Health Hospital ek extra option hai jahan doctors salaried hain aur PB customer ko 50% / 20% off milta hai."
► Position as a choice, never a restriction.
✓The 3-step recovery
1. Acknowledge: "Bilkul sahi sawal hai."
2. Answer with the pillar that kills the doubt (MG Road, OT/critical care, salaried doctors).
3. Bridge to action: "Main location WhatsApp kar deta hoon."
