Policybazaar runs its own multispeciality hospital - PB Health - at 5 NCR locations. Doctors are on fixed salary with zero revenue targets, so there is no incentive to overcharge. Your nearest centre is [HUB], and you and your family get exclusive discounts. Let me send you the PB Health app link.

Objection Battlecards

Page 2: Word-for-word counters & quick reference
Listen → Acknowledge → Answer → Push app
1Max / Fortis / Apollo jaisa hai kya?
Customer: "Bade hospitals jaisa hai kya? Doctors kaun hain?"
You: "Bilkul sahi sawal. Wahan har speciality ke senior specialists hain - cardio, ortho, gynae, paeds, surgery. Fark ye hai ki hamare doctors fixed salary pe hain, unka koi target nahi. Toh aapko wahi treatment milega jo zaruri hai - na faltu test, na zyada din admit. Aap app pe doctors aur specialities dekh kar khud decide kijiye."
► Same senior expertise + no billing incentive.
2Gurgaon toh bahut door hai
Customer: "Main Noida / Rohini / Faridabad mein hoon, Gurgaon kaun jayega?"
You: "Sir, hum sirf Gurgaon mein nahi hain - 5 centres hain: IFFCO Chowk, Golf Course Ext. Sector 62, Faridabad Sector 2, Noida Sector 50 aur Rohini. Aapke liye sabse paas [HUB] hai."
► ALWAYS name the hub nearest the customer first.
3Meri existing policy chalegi?
Customer: "Mera Star / HDFC / Care ka plan hai - wahan cashless hoga?"
You: "Cashless process Policybazaar ke saath integrated hai. Aap apni policy ka naam bata dijiye, main abhi system mein confirm karke batata hoon."
► Never guess - confirm insurer cashless status live.
4Sirf clinic hai kya?
Customer: "Wahan surgery / ICU / admission hota hai ya bas OPD?"
You: "Ye full multispeciality hospital hai - operation theatres, ICU, daycare procedures aur diagnostics, sab ek hi jagah. Consultation se lekar surgery aur admission tak."
► Say 'OT, ICU, daycare' explicitly.
5Discount kaise milega?
Customer: "Discount milega kaise? Family ko bhi milega?"
You: "Haan, aapko aur aapki immediate family ko. PB Health app pe apne Policybazaar wale mobile number se login kijiye aur wahin se booking kijiye - consultation, tests, checkups aur planned daycare pe discount milta hai."
► Walk them through the first app login on the call.
6App baad mein download kar lunga
Customer: "Abhi zarurat nahi, baad mein dekh lunga."
You: "Bas 1 minute lagega sir - main link bhej raha hoon, abhi khol ke dekh lijiye. Emergency ke time pe download karna mushkil hota hai; app mein hoga toh family ke liye bhi turant booking ho jayegi."
► Send the link while still on the call; confirm it opened.
7Insurance company ka hospital - wahin bhejoge?
Customer: "Aap apne hi hospital mein bhejna chahte ho na?"
You: "Bilkul nahi sir - aapki policy saare network hospitals mein valid hai, choice aapki hai. PB Health ek extra option hai jahan doctors salary pe hain aur aapko family discount milta hai."
► Position as a choice, never a restriction.
✓The 3-step recovery
1. Acknowledge: "Bilkul sahi sawal hai."
2. Answer with the pillar that kills the doubt (salary model, nearest hub, OT/ICU).
3. Bridge to action: "Main app ka link bhej deta hoon, aap khud dekh lijiye."
